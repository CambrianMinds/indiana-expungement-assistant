Legal Notice, Mission & Disclaimers

Why This Project Exists

I am an independent individual who believes that the criminal record expungement process under Indiana's Second Chance Law (IC § 35-38-9) should be an accessible, straightforward process for ordinary people.

Today, the system is unnecessarily complicated and intimidating, and many people who have earned a clean slate cannot afford thousands of dollars for private attorneys. This tool was built to level the playing field by providing automated pro se document preparation.

Crucial One-Shot Rule Warning (IC § 35-38-9-9(i))

Under Indiana Code § 35-38-9-9(i): “A person may file a petition for expungement of a conviction record under this chapter only one (1) time during any period of the person's life.”

IF YOU OMIT ANY CONVICTION, YOU CAN NEVER EXPUNGE IT LATER. All petitions across different counties must also be filed within 365 days (IC § 35-38-9-9(d)).
